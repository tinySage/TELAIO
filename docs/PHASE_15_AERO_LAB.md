# Phase 15 — Aerodynamics laboratory

Analysis → Aerodynamics. The roadmap's rule: *start with analytical models and documented coefficients; full CFD requires a proper computational backend.* This lab computes no flow. It works from an **aero map**, the way real aero data is used: coefficients measured against ride height by a wind tunnel or CFD campaign and imported with their provenance.

## Model (`src/engineering/aero.ts`)

- **Aero map:** ClA, CdA and aero balance (front share of downforce) on a grid of front × rear ride heights, with provenance and a source record (required for SOURCED).
- **Interpolation:** bilinear between map points. Outside the map the coefficients are **clamped to the edge and flagged, never extrapolated**.
- **Wing configurations:** named ΔClA / ΔCdA / Δbalance deltas applied on top of the map, each with its own provenance. The weakest provenance wins.
- **Forces:** F = ½ρv²·CA, giving downforce (split front/rear by balance), drag, L/D, drag power, and centre of pressure from the rear axle (balance × wheelbase).
- **Pitch sensitivity:** ∂ClA/∂front ride height, ∂ClA/∂rear ride height, and ∂balance/∂pitch.
- **Ground effect:** a sweep lowering the car uniformly, reporting where the map's downforce peaks. This is a property of the supplied map, not a physics prediction.

## Data in and out

- **CSV import** (paste or file) in long format: `front_mm, rear_mm, ClA, CdA, balance`. Validation catches non-numeric rows, duplicate or missing grid points, non-increasing axes, negative CdA, balance outside 0–1, and SOURCED maps without a source. Maps export back to CSV.
- **Send to braking lab** writes ClA, CdA, balance and air density into the braking inputs with their provenance, so the braking lab switches to its aero model.

## Not provided, on purpose

Airflow visualisation and CFD need a flow solver and mesh workflow. The lab says so on screen. The Phase 17 solver contract is where an external solver would connect.

## No invented data

Everything starts empty. **Demo** loads a map generated by formula with a plausible shape (downforce rising as the car lowers, then stalling; balance moving forward with nose-down pitch) and three wing configurations, all ILLUSTRATIVE, "not wind-tunnel, CFD or SF-24 data".

## Acceptance

`src/engineering/aero.test.ts` (12 tests, 97 total):
- [x] Interpolation is exact at nodes and bilinear between them; it clamps rather than extrapolates and flags it.
- [x] Map validation covers axes, grids, ranges and sources; the demo map validates.
- [x] CSV round-trips exactly; missing points and short rows are reported.
- [x] Forces follow ½ρv²·CA with the balance split, L/D, drag power and CoP; provenance is DERIVED from a SOURCED map.
- [x] Speed and density are needed for forces, not coefficients.
- [x] Wing deltas apply and downgrade provenance.
- [x] Sensitivities match the finite differences of a known map.
- [x] The demo map's ground-effect peak lies inside its range.
- [x] The braking handoff carries values and source, and the braking lab switches to the aero model.

Browser: the demo gives 12.1 kN downforce at 250 km/h with L/D 3.44. A pasted CSV with a missing point and no source is rejected with both reasons; the corrected CSV imports as SOURCED. A ride height outside the map is flagged as clamped. Sending to braking with an ILLUSTRATIVE wing selected correctly arrives as ILLUSTRATIVE.

## Limitations

- Straight-line, steady flow only: no yaw, roll, DRS transients, wake or dirty-air effects.
- The map is two-dimensional (front and rear ride height); speed and yaw dependence would need more axes.
- The centre of pressure is longitudinal only and comes from balance, not from the pressure distribution.
